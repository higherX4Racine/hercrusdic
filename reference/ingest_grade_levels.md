# Convert two-character grade codes into a factor of two-character grade levels

Convert two-character grade codes into a factor of two-character grade
levels

## Usage

``` r
ingest_grade_levels(.grade_digraphs)
```

## Arguments

- .grade_digraphs:

  `<chr>` two-character codes with many different levels of pre-k

## Value

a factor with all pre-k collapsed into "PK", then "KG" and "01"-"12"
